## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a focused, tactile social media ecosystem engineered around intentionality, razor-sharp editorial clarity, and natural composure. Designed to disrupt the visual noise and algorithmic exhaustion typical of legacy platforms, this system embraces a modern hybrid of **Organic Minimalism** and **Contemporary Editorial Structure**.

### Target Audience & Visual Tone
Targeted at creators, modern thinkers, visual communicators, and culturally active mobile-first users who demand fluid social communication without bloated ornamentation. The interface feels organic yet architecturally disciplined—blending calming botanical sage with grounded slate surfaces and vibrant emerald accent triggers.

### Design Movement & Ethos
- **Crisp Structural Discipline:** Generous breathing room, structural dividers, and razor-sharp content frames evoke high-end editorial layouts rather than infinite ad-filled feeds.
- **Natural Dynamic Vitality:** Tactile greens and muted slate shades reduce eye strain while infusing interactive moments with punchy kinetic energy.
- **Pill Geometry & Ergonomic Focus:** Pill-shaped navigation anchors, tactile floating action triggers, and rounded cards prioritize one-handed thumb interaction on mobile devices.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the botanical spectrum of the brand assets, balanced precisely across dark slate surfaces and soft mint highlights.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#66BF86` - Vibrant Fresh Green):** Key active triggers, verified badges, active navigation icons, primary call-to-action buttons, and post engagement highlights (likes, retweets, share confirmations).
- **Secondary (`#B7DEC7` - Soft Sage / Light Mint):** Subtle interactive fills, passive badges, active chip outlines, secondary pill borders, and high-readability muted text accents.
- **Tertiary (`#3D9151` - Deep Forest Green):** Hover and pressed states for primary buttons, progress indicators, selected segment fills, and deep-toned borders.
- **Neutral (`#3D4645` - Deep Slate Charcoal):** The root anchor for deep surfaces, cards, sheets, headers, and contextual container overlays.

### Dark Mode & Layering Guidelines
- **Canvas Root:** Deep Charcoal Slate (`#3D4645` modulated with deep ink `#1C2221` for baseline canvases).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Feed Cards & Top Bar):** `#2A3231` with low-opacity borders (`rgba(183, 222, 199, 0.12)`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Modals & Sheets):** `#353E3D`.
- **Text & Glyph Hierarchy:**
  - High Emphasis: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Crisp Light Sage (`#F0F8F3`).
  - Medium Emphasis: Soft Mint Mist (`#B7DEC7` at 80% opacity or `#A2B7AA`).
  - Disabled / Hairline: `#566361`.

## Typography

The typographical pair establishes a distinct rhythm: geometric, modern display headers paired with ultra-legible, humanist body curves.

- **Headline Font (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers structural cutting-edge punch to the platform branding, screen headers, profile usernames, and key navigational titles. Its geometric cuts mimic the brand mark's angular identity.
- **Body & Label Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures seamless readability across dense social post threads, comments, interactive forms, and micro-metadata badges.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- High-level metric counters (re-posts, followers, counts) utilize `Space Grotesk` medium weights for an editorial data aesthetic.
- Long-form thread posts strictly use `body-lg` with generous line-height (`26px`) to eliminate eye fatigue during deep feed reading sessions.
- Timestamps, tags, category handles, and pill button labels use `label-md` and `label-sm` with slight tracking expansions (`0.02em` - `0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid mobile consumption anchored on an 8pt/4pt mathematical spacing grid.

### Mobile-First Social Canvas
- **Viewport Constraints:** On mobile viewports (<640px), the feed operates with an edge-to-edge fluid container accompanied by `1rem` (`16px`) horizontal padding to keep text clear of physical screen bezels.
- **Feed Card Separation:** Standard post cards use an internal padding of `space-md` (`16px`) and vertical inter-card gap spacing of `space-sm` (`8px`) or direct low-contrast dividers.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (<640px):** Single column feed (100% width, max 600px centered), fixed bottom navigation bar with floating compose pill.
  - **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 2-column layout (Left narrow navigation rail, centered 640px content thread). Outer margin scales to `2rem`.
  - **Desktop (>1025px):** 3-column asymmetric layout (260px Navigation sidebar, 640px Centered Feed, 340px Trending/Search widget rail). Margins expand to `3rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive muddy shadows, using instead **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Tinted Glazes** and **Low-Contrast Outlines**.

### Elevation Scale
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#1E2524`. Flat background tone.
- **Surface Level 1 (Feed Cards, Navigation Bars):** `#2A3231` with a hairline stroke: `1px solid rgba(183, 222, 199, 0.12)`. No dropshadow.
- **Surface Level 2 (Thread Replies, Quoted Posts, Input Embeds):** `#323B3A` nested directly within Level 1 cards to construct distinct internal visual hierarchy.
- **Surface Level 3 (Drawers, Bottom Action Sheets, Dialogs):** `#3B4544` with an ambient diffused shadow: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(18, 24, 23, 0.65)`, rimmed with a top border in `rgba(102, 191, 134, 0.25)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Action Pills (Level 4):** Primary floating compose button uses an active radial halo: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(102, 191, 134, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 3 (Pill-shaped)** architecture to produce an inviting, tactile mobile touch environment that balances the sharper geometric cuts of the brand identity.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Primary and secondary action buttons, profile "Follow" controls, search bars, filter chips, story avatar status rings, and contextual tag indicators.
- **Content Cards & Containers (`rounded-xl` / `24px`):** Social feed cards, media attachments, modal dialogs, and nested thread cards.
- **Media Thumbnails (`rounded-lg` / `16px`):** Video players, inline quote blocks, and image grid carousels.
- **Avatars:** Strictly circular (`border-radius: 50%`) with an optional `2px` dynamic active ring colored in `#66BF86`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Full pill shape (`9999px`), background in `#66BF86` (Vibrant Fresh Green), text in `#1E2524` (Deep Slate Ink) using `label-lg`. Height: `44px` for touch target accessibility. Pressed state drops slightly to `#3D9151`.
- **Secondary Button:** Full pill shape, background transparent with `1.5px` border in `#B7DEC7` (Soft Sage) at 50% opacity; text in `#B7DEC7`.
- **Icon / Ghost Actions:** Circular touch zones (`40x40px`) with smooth icon color transition to `#66BF86` upon interaction.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with `space-sm` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding. Inactive: `#2A3231` background with text in `#A2B7AA`. Active: `#66BF86` background with dark contrast text `#1E2524`, or outlined in `#B7DEC7`.

### Social Feed Cards
- Background: Level 1 Slate (`#2A3231`).
- Border: `1px solid rgba(183, 222, 199, 0.1)`.
- Internal Layout: Top row features circular avatar (`42px`), username in `Space Grotesk` (`headline-sm`), handle & timestamp in `body-sm` (`#A2B7AA`), and trailing overflow icon.
- Content Area: `body-lg` text leading down to rounded media containers (`16px` radius).
- Bottom Action Row: Four inline icons (Reply, Repost/Cut, Like, Share) with count labels styled in `label-md`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `#232B2A`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(183, 222, 199, 0.15)`. Focus state transitions to `1.5px solid #66BF86` accompanied by a soft `rgba(102, 191, 134, 0.2)` outer ring.
- Shape: Full pill (`9999px`) for global search bars; `rounded-xl` (`16px`) for multi-line compose text fields.

### Lists & Thread Connectors
- Social replies feature a vertical connecting thread spine: `2px` solid line colored in `rgba(183, 222, 199, 0.18)` running between parent and child avatars.
- List items feature active feedback states (`#353E3D` background glaze on touch).

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- Checkboxes: `6px` rounded corners, border `2px solid #566361`. Checked state fills with `#66BF86` with a white checkmark.
- Toggles: Pill container (`48x28px`) in `#2A3231`, switching to `#3D9151` when enabled, with a sliding `#FFFFFF` circle knob.
